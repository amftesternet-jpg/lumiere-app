Why Lumière

Built for European celebrations

From Parisian weddings to Andalusian fiestas, Bavarian festivals to New York parties — one elegant platform.

Instant Photo Sharing
Guests scan a QR code and start uploading instantly — no app download required. Photos appear in the shared album in real time.
POV Story Reels
Guests record 15-second POV moments that auto-compile into a cinematic event reel — every perspective, one story.
4-Language Native UI
Full native support in French, German, Spanish, and English. Guests interact in their own language — automatically detected.
Guest Management
Invite lists, RSVP tracking, table assignments. Send digital invitations in multiple languages from one dashboard.
GDPR-First Privacy
Built for Europe. Data stored on EU servers, full GDPR compliance, photos auto-delete after your chosen retention period.
Printed Memories
Order premium photo books, wall prints, or digital keepsake albums — delivered anywhere in Europe within 5 days.
